Enquiries

When you send an enquiry through the website, we use the details you provide — your email address and the text of the message — only to correspond with you, prepare a quote and follow up on that enquiry. If you include a name or telephone number in the message, we treat it as part of the same enquiry.

The processing is based on steps taken before entering into a contract, or on the legitimate interest in replying to an enquiry we have received, depending on what you ask (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). The website does not send newsletters and does not use these details for advertising.

The email address and the message are needed if we are to reply through the form. Without them, the enquiry cannot be sent. You can also contact us by telephone. The telephone link opens the calling app on your device. The website does not record the call.

How long we keep information

The website does not keep its own database of enquiries. The message is sent by email and kept in the mailbox for as long as it is needed to reply and to follow up on the same enquiry. Where the correspondence becomes a contract or an accounting record, it is kept for the period required by applicable accounting and tax law. If sending fails, the message is not stored on the website.

When the form is submitted, the IP address is used for up to 60 seconds, only to limit repeat submissions, and is not written to a database. The language cookie is kept for 180 days, or until you delete it.

Recipients

We use the technical services needed to run and protect the website. Infrastructure and email providers may process data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — delivery and protection of the website, including the short-lived use of the IP address when the form is submitted;
  • Resend — sending the enquiry by email;
  • Yahoo — the mailbox alx.voynov@yahoo.com, once the message is delivered.

We do not sell personal data or pass it to third parties for advertising.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Resend and Yahoo may process the content of an enquiry in the United States. Cloudflare may process technical data outside the European Economic Area in order to deliver and protect the website. Any such transfer is made under Chapter V of the GDPR. The website configuration does not record a separate contractual transfer tool, such as standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

The website uses a cookie to remember the language you choose. Details are in the Cookie Policy. The website does not currently use analytics or marketing cookies.
